Accessibility

A centre accessible to everyone

Our physical access features and support options before your visit.

accessible

Step-free entrance

The main entrance has a ramp and an automatic door.

elevator

Lift

All floors are reached by a wheelchair-friendly lift.

local_parking

Accessible parking

Reserved parking spaces are located closest to the entrance.

meeting_room

Spacious examination rooms

Rooms leave enough space for a wheelchair to turn.

wc

Accessible toilet

Every floor has a wide toilet with grab bars.

Support before your visit

Write your special needs in the appointment note so our team can prepare.

Digital accessibility
This site is built with keyboard navigation, sufficient colour contrast, scalable text and screen-reader labels in mind. If you notice an access problem, please write to us.
