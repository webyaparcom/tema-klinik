FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about appointments, examinations and our centre.

How can I book an appointment?

You can fill in the form on the appointment page or call our centre. Your request is confirmed according to the physician’s schedule.

What should I bring to my examination?

Please bring your ID, any previous test and imaging results, and a list of the medicines you take regularly.

How early should I arrive?

We suggest arriving about 15 minutes before your appointment for registration.

Can I cancel or change my appointment?

Yes. Just let us know by phone or e-mail; if possible, please tell us a day in advance.

What should I do in an emergency?

In an emergency, please call 112. Our centre works by appointment and does not provide emergency-room services.

Is there a separate waiting area for children?

Yes, the paediatrics floor has a separate waiting corner with toys and books.

How is my personal health data protected?

Data is processed only for examination and appointment purposes, under Law No. 6698. On this demo site, what you type into forms is not sent to a server.

Is the centre accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes. There is a ramp, a lift and accessible parking; details are on the Accessibility page.

Write to us with your questions

Appointment requests are confirmed according to the physician’s schedule, and confirmation is sent by phone or e-mail. In an emergency, please call 112.
