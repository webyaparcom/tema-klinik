Berrak Medical Centre
Berrak Medical Centre is a fictional demo centre that aims to let patients arrive at their examination calm and prepared. Working by appointment, clear information and compliance with hygiene standards are our core principles.
Hygiene and sterilisation
Instruments are tracked with sterilisation records and rooms are cleaned after every patient.
Privacy
Examination rooms are private and your health information is kept confidential.
