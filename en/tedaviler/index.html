Departments and treatments

Which departments do we cover?

In every department the examination, required tests and follow-up plan are determined by the physician.

stethoscope

Internal Medicine

General health assessment for adults, follow-up of chronic conditions and routine check-up planning.

ecg_heart

Cardiology

Heart and vascular examination, ECG, stress testing and rhythm monitoring.

child_care

Paediatrics

Growth follow-up, vaccination schedule and infection examinations for babies and children.

pregnant_woman

Obstetrics and Gynaecology

Pregnancy follow-up, gynaecological examination and preventive health screening.

accessibility_new

Orthopaedics and Traumatology

Musculoskeletal complaints, sports injuries, and follow-up of fractures and dislocations.

visibility

Ophthalmology

Vision examination, eye pressure measurement and retinal assessment.

neurology

Neurology

Assessment of headache, dizziness and nervous-system complaints.

physical_therapy

Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation

Exercise and rehabilitation programmes planned after the physician’s assessment.

lab_research

Laboratory and Imaging

Blood and urine tests; ultrasound and X-ray services.

Process

From appointment to examination

A planned visit in four steps.

01

Appointment request

Fill in the form or call us.

02

Confirmation

Your request is confirmed according to the physician’s schedule.

03

Registration

Register about 15 minutes before your appointment.

04

Examination

Your physician listens, examines and explains the next steps.

Write to us with your questions

Appointment requests are confirmed according to the physician’s schedule, and confirmation is sent by phone or e-mail. In an emergency, please call 112.
